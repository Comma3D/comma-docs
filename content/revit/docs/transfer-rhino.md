---
title: "Transfer Rhino 2D / 3D"
weight: 110
---

**Ribbon** · Comma Rvt → Export → Transfer Rhino 2D / 3D

Transfer Rhino content into Revit from the **2D** or **3D** tab.

- **2D** transfers curves, filled regions, and supported annotations. Review the **Detail Curves**, **Filled Region**, and **Annotation Family** settings for the content you want to bring into the active view.
- **3D** transfers Rhino geometry into Revit families. Review the project and family settings before running the transfer.

After transfer, check the placement and scale in Revit.
