Where to find it: `commaMenu` → **Surface** → `uFlipSrf`.

Flips surface or Brep normals to align with a vector, a world axis, or a guide curve.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| X / Y / Z / Crv | Flip toward that axis or toward a guide curve. |

## Prompts

`Select surfaces to flip`; then pick a flip vector or guide curve.
