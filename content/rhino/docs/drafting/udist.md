Where to find it: `commaMenu` → **Drafting** → `uDist`.

Measures the distance between two points; optional bake and clipboard.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| BakeResult | No/Yes. |
| UnitDisplay | Document or Meters. |

## Prompts

`Start point`; `End point`.
