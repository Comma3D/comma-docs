Where to find it: `commaMenu` → **Drafting** → `uGap`.

Reports the min/max gap between two geometries. Pick a point for a local gap, or Enter to bake min/max.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| BakeResult / UnitDisplay | Bake and units. |

## Prompts

`Select Source…`; `Select Target…`; `Pick point on Source to measure gap (Enter = bake Min/Max)`.
