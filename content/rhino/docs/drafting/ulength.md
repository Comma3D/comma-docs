Where to find it: `commaMenu` → **Drafting** → `uLength`.

Sums the lengths of selected curves; optional bake as a text dot.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| BakeResult | No/Yes. |
| UnitDisplay | Document or Meters. |

## Prompts

`Select Curves`.
