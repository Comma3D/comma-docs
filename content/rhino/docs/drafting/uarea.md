Where to find it: `commaMenu` → **Drafting** → `uArea`.

Totals the area of surfaces, meshes, hatches, or closed curves.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| BakeResult / UnitDisplay | Bake and units. |

## Prompts

`Select Objects for Area`.
