Where to find it: `commaMenu` → **Drafting** → `uDiffZ`.

Reports ΔX / ΔY / ΔZ between two points.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| BakeResult / UnitDisplay | Bake and units. |
| AxisX / AxisY / AxisZ | Which deltas to emphasise. |

## Prompts

`First Point`; `Second Point`.
