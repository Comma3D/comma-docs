Where to find it: `commaMenu` → **Doc** → `uSort`.

Opens the floating sort-order toolbar. Sort and bake actions stay on the toolbar (Select, Clear, X/Y/Z, Rad, Crv, Rev, Group, Loft, Nurbs, Poly).

## Limits

Free/Trial: `uSort` is Pro-gated.
