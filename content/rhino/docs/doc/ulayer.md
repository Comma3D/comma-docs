Where to find it: `commaMenu` → **Doc** → `uLayer`.

Opens or toggles the Comma Layer dock panel after the licence gate. Layer actions stay in the panel (including the Advance layer-command flyout).

## Limits

Free/Trial: the layer panel is Pro-gated. When the panel does open on a limited plan, the layer list is truncated to three layers.
