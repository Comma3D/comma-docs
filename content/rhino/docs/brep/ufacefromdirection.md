Where to find it: `commaMenu` → **Brep** → `uFaceFromDirection`.

Extracts Brep faces whose normals align with a direction within an angle tolerance.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Reverse | No/Yes. |
| BothDirections | No/Yes. |

## Prompts

`Select brep to extract faces`; direction points (Enter for Z-axis); `Angle tolerance in degrees`.
